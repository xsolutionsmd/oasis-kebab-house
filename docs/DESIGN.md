# Design: Oasis — classic editorial redesign

September 30, 2026 (abdul-dev). Brief from the owner: more professional and classy, a balanced and restrained palette rather than colorful, fewer words, more intuitive ordering, every image and the hero video looking intentional. This supersedes the Tikkaville visual treatment below; ordering, reservation, sender and membership behavior are unchanged on the server.

- **Reference direction.** Inspo/Stitch were not available in this cloud session and the restaurant's own site (oasiskebabhouse.com) was blocked by the network policy, so the fallback was a public-web direction: editorial restaurant sites in the vein of Dishoom — ivory ground, a serif display face, generous whitespace and one quiet accent — combined with the proven online-ordering pattern (sticky category chips, persistent order summary, bottom order bar on phones). Menu, hours and photos come from the existing repository sources; public review sites were read for tone only. No review quotes, ratings or new claims were added. "Halal" appears on the restaurant's own entrance sign (entrance.webp) and on its menu.
- **Palette** from the logo, warmed up after owner feedback asking for more colour and life: ivory `#faf7f1`, ink `#1c2530`, Samarkand blue `#1d4e6e`/lapis `#1a4866`/deep lapis `#0f2a3d`, saffron `#d9a441` for primary actions on dark grounds, warm sand `#f3e7d3` bands and page headers. Blue bands carry a faint eight-point-star tile lattice (inline SVG, ~8% opacity).
- **Type.** Self-hosted Cormorant Garamond (headings) and Inter (body/UI), OFL. Oswald removed.
- **Hero video** (Instagram reel C1z2xlPLGG9). Owner feedback: full-screen looked "super zoomed and shaky". The clip is now the calmest 10 seconds of the footage (measured by frame-to-frame motion), slowed to ~70% with interpolated frames and blended into a seamless 12.9 s loop: 53% less motion per frame, 2.9 MB. Desktop: the hero fills the screen, with copy on the star pattern and the portrait video edge to edge on the right at its own proportions (no enlargement). Phones: the video fills the screen behind the copy, where portrait fits naturally. Playlist-ready (`HERO_CLIPS`); no visible pause control (keyboard focus only).
- **Dish photos.** Only real Oasis photos, matched where the dish is certain: Instagram (plov, samsa, shrimp, grill) and the round photos on the restaurant's April 2024 printed menu (lagman, shurpa, shish kebab special; salad photo used only as the Salads category image because the exact salad is unclear). Menu categories show a round photo beside the heading. Items without a confirmed photo stay text-only; no stock or generated food images.
- **No test labelling in the interface.** The "website preview / test order" notices are removed at the owner's request. The server still runs with DEMO_MODE until the restaurant confirms operating settings (noindex; email subjects marked Demo).
- **Menu navigation.** Search and categories live in a left side panel. On desktop it collapses to a slim rail (remembered per browser); on phones a "Sections & search" button slides it in over the menu. Category links scroll so the heading lands 16px below the header (below the floating button on phones), and hold the highlight until the scroll settles. Items are rounded white cards; the + button no longer jumps on hover (its hover scale used `transform`, which overwrote the vertical-centering transform).
- **Checkout entry.** The sticky "Your order" panel is gone. A floating Checkout button (count, total) appears only once the order has something in it, on every page except checkout and status pages, and goes straight to checkout; the header bag still opens the editable order drawer. The large "Menu" title was removed (kept as a screen-reader heading) so the menu starts right below the header.
- **About page** (`/about`, linked in the header and mobile nav). Intro with the dining-room photo; "The Uzbek table" with six real Oasis photos (plov, samsa, lagman, kebabs, non, shurpa) and short general notes on each dish; a dastarkhan/hospitality note with patio, entrance and Samarkand photos. Restaurant facts are limited to what it publishes (halal, Pikesville, Samarkand plov, dining room and patio). No invented history, founders or awards. The UNESCO reference is to "Palov culture and tradition" (Uzbekistan, 2016).
- **Item view.** Clicking a dish opens a large view (inspired by the click-to-enlarge pattern on restaurant ordering menus): photo filling the left half beside the details on desktop, photo on top on phones. Dishes without a photo keep the compact view.
- **About video.** "Inside Oasis" embeds the public YouTube walkthrough of the dining room (watch?v=72h_LgxQ5W4, posted by Rustam Azimov). Click-to-load via youtube-nocookie.com; the CSP allows only that frame source. Not described as the owner speaking, because that couldn't be confirmed.
- **Scroll fix.** The menu's active-category tab used scrollIntoView, which also moved the page and fought the reader's scrolling (scroll steps jumped back on desktop). It now scrolls only the tab strip sideways.
- **Motion (owner: "smooth, slick, modern").** Cross-page fades via the View Transitions API (header stays put), header tightens with a soft shadow once scrolled, sections and dish rows rise in once as they enter the viewport, cards lift on hover, dialogs pop and the order drawer slides in. All of it is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion; the menu list itself is not animated so search and category jumps stay instant.
- **More video.** Instagram's public embeds expose no video files and listing posts needs a login, so the plov reel remains the only clip. Additional clips need to be supplied by the restaurant.
- **Background and ornament from the restaurant's own site** (oasiskebabhouse.com, "Massively" template): its faint gray Islamic star lattice (`bg-pattern.jpg`, cut to a seamless 519×567 tile, `media/bg-pattern.webp`) behind every page, and its gold mandala (`overlay-mandala.png`, resized, `media/mandala.webp`) as a small ornament above Popular dishes. Primary colour is the site's blue #0a5688.
- **One place per fact.** Opening hours live in the footer and on the Visit page only (hidden from the Visit page footer). Address/phone/directions: footer and Visit page. "Pay in store" appears once, in the pickup note. Removed: header open-status, hero facts row, home "three steps" band, home visit band, menu meta line.
- **Only functional content** (owner rule: nothing a restaurant site doesn't need). Home is hero (name, one factual line: halal, location, pickup/dine-in; Order pickup / Reserve) → popular dishes with quick add → footer (address, phone, directions, hours). Removed: story section, gallery strip, taglines/eyebrows, reservation side panel, visit-page FAQ, footer logo/blurb/duplicate nav. Gallery page stays reachable by URL only (no nav link). Kept: allergy note on the menu, demo notice, pickup note.
- **Ordering.** Items without options add in one tap (toast with "View order"); items with required choices open the option dialog. Rows show "N in order". Desktop keeps a sticky order summary beside the menu; tablet/phone show a bottom order bar. Drawer and summaries allow quantity changes and removal. Checkout uses day chips (only open days), preselects the earliest time and skips to the next day when today is full, numbered steps, and an optional "remember my details on this device" (browser-only storage, documented in Privacy). The place-order button shows the total.
- **Reservations:** party-size chips, day chips, time chips, then details.
- **Status page:** progress tracker (Received → Confirmed → Preparing → Ready → Collected, or Requested → Confirmed → Seated), directions/call actions, auto-refresh every 30 s until a final state.
- **Header:** logo, four links, open-now status, Order pickup and order button; phones get a slide-in navigation sheet. No public admin link.
- Accessibility: native dialogs, real radio inputs behind chips, visible brass focus rings, 16px inputs on phones, reduced motion honored.
